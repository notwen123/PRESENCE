PRESENCE. Proof a human was here.

AI agents can now operate phones, so anything that checks for taps can be farmed. PRESENCE verifies that a real person physically completed a session on a real device (server-timed, hash-chained, knock check-ins the accelerometer must feel) and records it once on Monad. Any contract can ask isPresent().
