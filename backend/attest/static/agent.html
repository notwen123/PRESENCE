Knock to Approve

An AI agent can spend a little on its own. Anything bigger waits for its human to physically knock on their phone. The phone signs that exact transaction, and the Monad contract checks the signature itself.

Set up this phone

Lay the phone face-down, then knock

Tap Start, put the phone face-down on a table, and knock twice on its back. It buzzes when it feels you.
